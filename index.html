House values

What we ask of each other

Curfew & Silent hours, kept

9 PM–7 AM 24/7 by everyone and for everyone.

Shared spaces, shared upkeep

Store room and common room stay usable because residents leave them the way they found them.

An open door to staff

House Guidelines at a Glance

  • Residents should stay and maintain silence from 9:00 PM till 7:00 AM
  • Zero tolerance to smoking & drugs
  • Zero tolerance to ragging and bullying
  • No open flames or unregistered appliances
  • Recycling sorted at the bin station
  • No visiters allowed unless under genuine circumstances
  • No tresspassing
  • Pets not allowed
